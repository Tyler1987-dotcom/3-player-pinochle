Privacy Policy

3 Player Pinochle for macOS

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Introduction

Tyler McQuade ("we," "our," or "the developer") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use 3 Player Pinochle for macOS.

Information Collection

3 Player Pinochle does not collect or transmit personal information. The app is designed for card-game entertainment, and gameplay data, including scores and statistics, is handled locally on your Mac.

Gameplay Data

Game scores, statistics, and preferences may be processed or stored locally on your Mac. This information is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

Third-Party Services

3 Player Pinochle does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

Internet Connection

The app does not transmit personal information over the internet. Gameplay is intended to be handled locally on your Mac.

Children's Privacy

3 Player Pinochle does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Data Security

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, it does not maintain a developer-operated database of users' personal information. Users should keep their Mac and its local data secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or information-handling practices change. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
